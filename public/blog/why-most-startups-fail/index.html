Most startups die. Some never find product-market fit. Some break up over co-founder fights. Some run out of money.

Pick the one you think kills the most.

It's you. That's the first page of The Book of Clarity by Paras Chopra. Most startups never die in the market. They die at the idea stage, inside the founder's head, before anyone else ever sees them.

The 2025 deal valued Wingify at about $200 million, roughly ₹1,700 crore. Against the ₹700 he started with, that is about 2.4 crore times.

The number is fun. It isn't the point. The point is that a 15-year-old with four dead websites didn't stop at the idea stage. He kept shipping small things until one worked.
