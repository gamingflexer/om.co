The desire to see family didn't move. Only the way we fulfil it did.

That's the idea behind a chapter in The Book of Clarity by Paras Chopra, who co-founded Wingify. It's called "No market is ever done", and I'm reading the book one idea a day.

The point

Know the desire, not the solution

You know who the smartest entrepreneurs are? Not the ones who know how to make the solutions. The ones who know exactly which desire people want fulfilled.

What people care about is their desires. Not how they fulfil them today.

The good news

That means no market is ever done

If a desire has many solutions, and the solutions keep changing, then there's always room for a better one. Good news for founders.

The proof

We stopped going to libraries

Do you remember when we stopped going to libraries and started googling? Then we stopped going through all those web pages. Now we ask ChatGPT and Gemini for answers. Same need. Each time, a better answer.

If an investor asks

"Do you have any competition?"

Never say no. No matter how unique your product is, there is already something that satisfies the need. Even Excel competes with your CRM.

Remember

Solutions come and go. Desires stay.

Boots, cars, planes, libraries, even Google: each one is a solution for a desire. Let some time pass.
