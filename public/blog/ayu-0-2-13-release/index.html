Ayu 0.2.13: everything we shipped this week

▶ Watch the 55-second reel

We shot this one at the yellow wall, passing one mic between three of us. It starts with a mistake: "This is everything we are shipping this week." "But we already shipped, right?" "…Everything we already shipped this week."

Here is the list. One feature each, no slides. I build Ayu, a health records app for Indian families, and 0.2.13 is the build all of this rides on.

A new onboarding for the family plan

Health records are a family thing in India. One person holds the files for parents, kids, a spouse. The new onboarding says that upfront: keep all your loved ones' medical records in one place, and switch between profiles.

Samyakta

Our first paying Plus user

Someone paid for Ayu Plus. We have wanted to write that line for a long time. Thank you. The video has confetti, and it is real: we rolled our hands and it went off.

Om

Password-protected PDFs

Many lab reports and discharge summaries come locked with a password. Ayu now supports them: it asks for the password and opens the file. No more unlocking it somewhere else and uploading it again.

Onkar

Connect your Whoop

You can now connect your Whoop band with Ayu, so your wearable data and your records live in one place.

Onkar

Trends in chat

Every document you upload, we extract the trends out of it. Now you can compare those trends right in the chat, and compare different types of trends with each other.

Om

ABHA will be live on Ayu soon. It is not out yet, so this is a promise with a date still to come. I will post when it ships.
